Environment Management

Our Environmental Principles

Eight pillars guiding every step of our recycling operations — from intake of end-of-life vessels to final material recovery, with marine ecosystems always in mind.

01

Commitment to Environmental Sustainability

  • Dedicated to minimizing the environmental impact of ship recycling.
  • Adheres to internationally recognized standards — Hong Kong International Convention (HKC) and ISO 14001.
02

Eco-Friendly Recycling Processes

  • Maximum material recovery and minimal waste — supporting a circular economy.
  • Advanced techniques to manage hazardous materials like asbestos, lead-based paints, and residual oils.
03

Pollution Control Measures

  • Stringent measures prevent pollution of air, water, and soil during dismantling.
  • State-of-the-art containment systems prevent runoff and ensure safe disposal of contaminants.
04

Hazardous Material Disposal

  • Safe handling, storage, and disposal of hazardous waste in full regulatory compliance.
  • All hazardous materials treated or disposed at licensed facilities — zero environmental harm.
05

Compliance with International Standards

  • Aligned with the Basel Convention and other global agreements for sustainable recycling.
  • Active members of green recycling initiatives championing responsible practices.
06

Biodiversity Protection

  • Strict environmental controls protect nearby marine and terrestrial ecosystems from recycling impacts.
  • Active care for coastal habitats, fisheries, and surrounding wildlife corridors.
07

Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

  • Regular environmental audits and monitoring identify improvement opportunities.
  • Ongoing investment in workforce training for environmentally sound operations.
08

Community Engagement

  • Working closely with local communities to promote awareness about sustainable practices.
  • Supporting environmental conservation initiatives across the region.

Waste Management

Responsible Practices for a Sustainable Future

From segregation to zero-waste — every stream is engineered for accountability, traceability, and global compliance.

01

Comprehensive Waste Segregation

  • Robust process separates steel, non-ferrous metals, plastics, and hazardous substances efficiently.
  • Each material is categorized for recycling, reuse, or safe disposal — minimizing landfill.
02

Hazardous Waste Handling

  • Asbestos, lead-based paints, residual fuels, and PCBs handled per international standards.
  • Trained specialists use advanced techniques — zero harm to workers and environment.
03

Recycling & Reuse Focus

  • Over 90% of materials from dismantled ships recycled — supporting circular-economy goals.
  • Reusable components like machinery, spare parts, and fittings are refurbished and repurposed.
04

Pollution Prevention Measures

  • Advanced containment systems and impermeable surfaces prevent soil and water contamination.
  • Effluent treatment plants treat and recycle wastewater (GEPIL standards).
05

Compliance with International Standards

  • Aligned with HKC, Basel Convention, IMO and ILO guidelines, plus GEPIL norms.
  • Operating procedures follow MoEF&CC and SPCB regulations for sustainable recycling.
06

Monitoring & Documentation

  • All waste streams tracked and documented for accountability and regulatory compliance.
  • Regular audits and inspections continually improve our waste management systems.
07

Partnering with Certified Facilities

  • Collaborations with licensed waste treatment and disposal facilities.
  • Ensures proper treatment, transport, and disposal of hazardous and non-recyclable waste.
08

Commitment to Zero Waste

  • Ultimate goal: achieve zero waste through continuous innovation.
  • Optimizing recycling and waste-management processes year on year.
