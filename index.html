Our Company

Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship Breaking Unit) LLP and Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers Pvt. Ltd. is involved in the business since ship breaking activity started at Alang in 1983.

Established Legacy

Ship breaking business was first carried out by our group under the name of Madhav Industrial Corporation till 1997.

Sachdeva Steel Products

In 1997, we started the business under the name Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship Breakers) and till date 36 ships have been recycled.

Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers

To further expand the business, we established another unit in 1998 and till date 35 ships have been recycled.

OUR LEGACY

Sachdeva Group Journey

Since 1983

Our Foundation

Since our inception in 1983, Sachdeva Group has been a pioneer in the ship recycling industry, committed to sustainable practices and innovative solutions.

1983-1997

Early Years

Our founder established the group with a single ship recycling yard, driven by environmental stewardship and maritime expertise. The early years focused on building a skilled workforce and modern facilities.

2000s

Growth and Expansion

By the early 2000s, our commitment to sustainability earned us a trusted global reputation. We expanded operations under Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers and implemented strict HSE standards.

2010s

Innovation & Compliance

We continuously improved our processes to comply with international regulations such as the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships.

Today & Beyond

Community & Future Vision

We invest in workforce training and community development. Today, Sachdeva Group stands as a leader in sustainable ship recycling, committed to innovation and long-term environmental responsibility.
